Customer Conversion & Campaign Performance Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes customer conversion behavior using the Bank Marketing Dataset. The objective is to identify funnel drop-offs, evaluate campaign effectiveness, and generate actionable recommendations to improve conversion rates.

Objective

Analyze Funnel Performance

Tool

Power BI

Dataset

Bank Marketing Dataset

Focus

Customer Conversion

Dashboard Preview

Key Insights

  • Lead quality has a greater impact on conversions than campaign volume.
  • Significant funnel drop-offs occur during early customer engagement.
  • High-converting customer segments drive the majority of successful outcomes.
  • Targeted outreach improves customer acquisition efficiency.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize high-converting customer segments.
  • Improve lead qualification processes.
  • Optimize campaign outreach strategies.
  • Reduce funnel drop-offs through personalized engagement.
